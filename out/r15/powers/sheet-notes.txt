# What came out (all measured on these voxel solids; bodies were counted by shaving the solid and counting what is left, then looked at by eye)
1. The flat plane is right: the cut at z = 0 is the flat set of z^n + c for every power and every c drawn here (601 x 601 points each, 0 points differ, 10 cases).
2. The number of bodies follows the power. Julia sets, c at the centre of a period-2 body: n bodies round the main body in the plane, n x n in all.
     Power 2: 4 (two wings in the plane, two legs below).   Power 3: 9 (3 in the plane, 3 above, 3 below).
     Power 4: 16 in four rows of four at c = (0.5, 0.866, 0); at c = -1 they have run together into one ridged lump.   Power 5: 25 in five rows of five (23 of them still there at a deeper shave).
3. Mandelbrot solids: in the plane the flat set's n - 1 lobes. Off the plane there are horns, fins and ridges, not lobes. I found no count there that follows the power.
4. Power 3 is the best looking: a rounded main body with round bodies on it in several directions and small knobs on those. It is mirror-symmetric above and below
     (0 voxels differ), and its upright cut at x = 0 is the flat set a second time (0 points differ).
5. Power 2 is the weak one. It is lopsided (c = -1: volume 0.19 above the plane, 0.61 below; it reaches down to z = -2.4 and up only to 0.66): thin wings and legs, flat faces.
     Closer looks (part 3) show the same picture at every size: straight smooth wedges where two bodies meet (c = -1), a smooth skin above the middle body,
     and for the rabbit flat faces with loose thin flakes round them. No smaller round bodies on the bodies at power 2.
6. The upright cut at y = 0 has straight edges at every power. For powers 3 and 5 the Mandelbrot solid's cut there is a plain square with no buds at all.
7. One piece or shards: the Mandelbrot solids hold 99.6 to 99.9 % of their volume in one piece at this grid, the Julia sets 97 to 100 %, except c = -1 at power 2 (85 %):
     its two wings touch the middle body in one point each, which voxels cannot show. The rabbit's flakes are loose at every size I looked at (1,400 to 1,950 pieces in a cube).
8. Next to the other layout of the same rule (part 4, power 2 only): the other layout gives rounder bodies and is nearly even above and below (c = -1: 0.47 above, 0.46 below).
9. Shapes under repetition (largest stretch divided by smallest after 6 steps, median / 99 %): this layout 2.9 / 23, the other layout 1.9 / 21, Mandelbulb 1.4 / 53,
     Sphere inversions one plane 7e14 (grows without end). Both pillow layouts stay bounded; so does the Mandelbulb at power 2, which was not expected.
